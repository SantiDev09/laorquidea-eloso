South Africa Trade Mission 2026

Why this trade mission?

Colombia and South Africa currently trade very little specialty coffee directly. Most volumes travel through European or North American intermediaries before reaching African roasters — adding cost, time and distance between the farm and the cup.

La Orquídea y el Oso is joining the South Africa Trade Mission 2026 to open a direct route: our specialty, organic coffee from Inzá, Tierradentro, presented straight to buyers, distributors and roasters in South Africa and West Africa.

  • A direct commercial bridge between Colombian growers and African buyers, without unnecessary intermediaries.
  • Samples, technical sheets and export-ready lots presented in person to the trade.
  • A long-term relationship with African roasters, not a one-off shipment.
