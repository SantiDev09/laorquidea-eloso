Trazabilidad

From Forest to Cup — Five Stages, Every One Documented

Every container we export carries coffee from dozens of individual producers in Inzá, Tierradentro. No blending that erases who grew what. Here's exactly how a lot moves from tree to export document:

1
Growers

Real families, each one named — scored and tracked through the O&O Index from the very first visit.

2
Harvest & Processing

Ripe cherries hand-picked, one by one, by lot, variety and microclimate — then processed with care at the producer's own farm, or alongside our team at the O&O Origin Hub.

3
Laboratory

Cupping controls and builds every lot — the same process that improves the farm behind it, harvest after harvest.

4
Preparation

Milled, sorted and double-packed with a coded report per lot — ready to travel, ready to trace.

5
Export

Documented, lot-traceable shipment — ready for buyers in South Africa and West Africa.

Specialty coffee is only truly special if it protects its origin.

SOUTH AFRICA TRADE MISSION 2026

South Africa Trade Mission 2026

Colombia and South Africa currently trade very little specialty coffee directly. Most volumes travel through European or North American intermediaries before reaching African roasters — adding cost, time and distance between the farm and the cup.

La Orquídea y el Oso is joining the South Africa Trade Mission 2026 to open a direct route to explore and connect authenticity: specialty, organic coffee from Inzá, Tierradentro, presented directly to specialty buyers, distributors and roasters in South Africa and West Africa.

  • A direct commercial bridge between Colombian growers and African buyers — no unnecessary intermediaries.
  • Samples, technical sheets and export-ready lots presented in person.
  • A long-term relationship with African roasters, not a one-off shipment.
