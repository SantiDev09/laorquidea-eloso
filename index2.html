Lot by Lot

Trazabilidad

From forest to cup: six stages, one traceable lot

Every container we export carries coffee from dozens of individual producers in Inzá, Tierradentro — quality with artisanal complexity that no monoculture can replicate, tracked from the tree to the export document.

1
Growers

Real families, each one named — scored and tracked through the O&O Index from the very first visit.

2
Harvest

Ripe cherries hand-picked by lot, variety and microclimate — each harvest kept separate, never mixed at collection

3
Processing

Processed with care at the producer's own farm, or alongside our team at the O&O Origin Hub — whichever the lot calls for.

4
Laboratory

Cupping controls and builds every lot — the same process that improves the farm behind it, harvest after harvest.

5
Preparation

Milled, sorted and double-packed with a coded report per lot — ready to travel, ready to trace.

6
Export

Documented, lot-traceable shipment — ready for buyers in South Africa, Ghana and Senegal.

SOUTH AFRICA TRADE MISSION 2026

Specialty Coffee — South Africa Ghana and Senegal Trade Mission 2026

Colombia trades very little specialty coffee directly with South Africa, Ghana and Senegal. Most volumes travel through European or North American intermediaries before reaching African roasters — adding cost, time and distance between the farm and the cup.

La Orquídea y el Oso is joining Ella Exporta África 2026 to open a direct route: specialty coffee from Inzá, Tierradentro, presented directly to buyers, distributors and roasters across all three markets.

  • A direct commercial bridge between Colombian growers and West and Southern African buyers — no unnecessary intermediaries.
  • Samples, technical sheets and export-ready lots presented in person. A long-term relationship with African roasters, not a one-off shipment.
  • A long-term relationship with African roasters, not a one-off shipment.
